MY TEACHING METHOD

Learn French by actually using it.

Grammar and vocabulary support communication. The main focus is speaking, listening and getting feedback you can put into practice.

01

Speak

Try a realistic conversation or role-play adapted to your goals.

02

Get feedback

Identify the expressions, pronunciation points and mistakes worth correcting.

03

Understand

Learn a useful pattern or explanation without getting lost in unnecessary theory.

04

Reuse

Try again, use the correction and build confidence through repetition.

You'll have room to make mistakes, ask questions and practice at a pace that works for you.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about online French conversation lessons

Do I need to speak French already?

Live in French is designed mainly for learners from A1 to B2. If you're unsure of your level, we can discuss your needs during the free discovery call.

Are these lessons just free conversation?

No. You'll speak a lot, but lessons have clear goals. I prepare relevant situations and give targeted feedback so you can understand and reuse what you learn.

Will we study grammar?

Yes, when it helps you communicate. We work on useful grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation in the context of real conversations rather than making grammar exercises the main activity.

Can I take lessons before moving to France?

Absolutely. We can practice the conversations you'll need for housing, appointments, work and social life before you arrive.

How long are the lessons?

Each private lesson lasts 50 minutes. The Live in French program includes 16 lessons over 8 weeks, usually two per week.

Where do the online lessons take place?

Lessons and discovery calls can take place on Google Meet. You'll receive the practical details when you book.
