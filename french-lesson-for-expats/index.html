YOUR LIFE IN FRANCE

Practical French for the moments that matter.

Living in France means more than learning vocabulary. It means asking the right questions, understanding the answers and feeling comfortable in everyday interactions.

01 · BEFORE YOU MOVE

Prepare for your arrival

Build the language skills you'll need during your first days in France.

  • Ask about an apartment or a rental appointment
  • Introduce yourself and explain your situation
  • Ask for directions and practical information
02 · YOUR FIRST WEEKS

Handle everyday tasks

Practice the conversations that make daily life easier.

  • Make or change an appointment
  • Explain a problem in a shop or with a delivery
  • Ask someone to repeat or clarify information
03 · FEELING CONNECTED

Build a social life

Go beyond short exchanges and feel more comfortable meeting people.

  • Chat with neighbors and acquaintances
  • Join a conversation over coffee or dinner
  • Share experiences, preferences and opinions
04 · AT WORK

Take part in workplace conversations

Use French in the informal and practical situations you encounter with colleagues.

  • Make small talk during a coffee break
  • Explain a task or describe a problem
  • Ask questions and contribute to discussions

These are examples, not a fixed syllabus. Your lessons are adapted to your goals, current level and the situations you actually face.

BEFORE OR AFTER YOUR MOVE

Learn French wherever you are in your journey.

You don't have to wait until you arrive in France to start practicing. And if you've lived here for a while, it's never too late to feel more comfortable using French.

What would help you most?

  • Moving soon? Rehearse the conversations you're likely to have in your first weeks.
  • Already in France? Work on situations that still feel stressful or confusing.
  • Working in English? Build confidence using French outside meetings and in everyday social exchanges.
  • Know some French but hesitate? Practice responding without planning every sentence in advance.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about learning French for life in France

Should I start French lessons before moving to France?

You can. We can practice useful situations such as asking about housing, introducing yourself, making appointments and getting help, so you feel better prepared when you arrive.

Can I take lessons if I already live in France?

Absolutely. We can work on real situations you've experienced, whether that's speaking with neighbors, understanding everyday conversations or using French with colleagues.

Do I need to be fluent to start?

Not at all. Complete beginners (A0) are welcome. We can start with essential words, simple expressions and guided everyday interactions, then gradually build your speaking confidence. Lessons are adapted for A0–B2 learners, and we'll discuss your goals during the free discovery call.

Will the lessons cover French paperwork and administration?

We can practice the language used in everyday appointments, questions and requests for clarification. These are French language lessons, not legal or administrative advice.

Are the lessons only for people working in France?

No. Lessons can be useful for international professionals, partners, newcomers and other adults preparing for or already living in France. Activities are tailored to your goals.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons take place online, usually on Google Meet. Each private session lasts 50 minutes. The Live in French program includes 16 lessons over 8 weeks, typically twice per week.
