Cookies & Trackers Policy

Last updated: November 2026

We use optional Google Analytics 4 audience-measurement cookies only if you accept them. You can refuse or change your choice at any time.

1. What are cookies and trackers?

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies can also store or access information on a device and are commonly referred to as trackers.

3. Your choices

On your first visit, you can accept or refuse optional analytics cookies. Refusing does not prevent you from using our website. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so we do not ask on every page view.

You can reopen the choices at any time using the button below. If you withdraw consent, we stop loading analytics on subsequent page views. You may also delete existing analytics cookies using your browser settings.

Google Analytics 4 is provided by Google. For more information about Google's handling of data, see Google's Privacy Policy. Analytics cookie retention varies by cookie and configuration; for example, Google's default _ga cookie duration is typically up to two years unless settings or browser restrictions shorten it.

4. Discovery Call booking

When you click the booking button, you are redirected to Calendly, an external appointment scheduling service. Calendly may use cookies or similar technologies on its own website. For information about how your booking data is processed, please see our Privacy Policy.

5. Browser controls

You can view, delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary technical cookies may affect the operation or security of some services.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the website begins using additional cookies, trackers or embedded third-party services.

7. Contact

For questions about cookies, trackers or privacy, contact contact@frenchwithjo.com.
