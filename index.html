A PRACTICAL METHOD

You learn French by using French.

Grammar matters — but it is a tool. The goal is to communicate clearly, naturally and confidently.

01

SPEAK

Use French in real situations adapted to your level.

02

CORRECT

Identify the mistakes that really matter for communication.

03

UNDERSTAND

Understand the useful rule, pattern or pronunciation point.

04

REUSE

Use the correction again immediately until it feels more natural.

One possible 50-minute lesson structure — this is only an example. Every lesson is adapted to your level, goals and what comes up naturally during the session.

0–5 min

Warm-up

Natural conversation to get comfortable and start using French right away.

5–10 min

Reuse

Bring back useful vocabulary, expressions or corrections from the previous lesson.

10–15 min

Useful input

Introduce the language, vocabulary or pronunciation needed for today's goal.

15–30 min

Speak & practice

The core of the lesson: conversation, role-play, storytelling, discussion or real-life situations.

30–35 min

Correction

Focus on a few important mistakes, patterns or pronunciation points.

35–43 min

Reuse again

Use the corrected language immediately in a new situation.

43–47 min

Pronunciation

Work on one or two sounds, rhythm or clarity issues noticed during the lesson.

47–50 min

Feedback & next step

Review progress, key takeaways and what to work on next.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Use lesson time for what matters most.

An app or AI can explain a grammar rule. Our time together is better used for interaction, feedback, pronunciation and learning how to express what you actually want to say.

✓ More speaking, less passive listening
✓ Corrections based on your real mistakes
✓ Pronunciation integrated into conversation
✓ Short grammar explanations when useful
✓ Writing feedback and progress tracking
✓ Real-life situations instead of isolated drills
