A PRACTICAL METHOD

You learn French by using French.

Grammar matters — but it is a tool. The goal is to communicate clearly, naturally and confidently.

01

SPEAK

Use French in situations adapted to your level and goals.

02

CORRECT

Focus on the mistakes and pronunciation points that matter most.

03

UNDERSTAND

Understand the useful rule, pattern or expression behind the correction.

04

REUSE

Use the corrected language again until it starts to feel more natural.

An app or AI can explain a grammar rule. Our time together is better used for interaction, feedback, pronunciation and learning how to express what you actually want to say.
